## Scope
Blog home (post index), post detail, tag and author pages, post editor. Mode: Read (index, detail, tag, author); Operate (editor). Drafts live in MagicPath as one frame per screen.

## Audience and job
External developers arriving from search, and interplug teammates sharing knowledge. They find a post, read long technical text and code comfortably, and see who wrote it. Teammates write posts in markdown.

## Constraints
Avoid neon/cyberpunk, stock blog card grids, hard-to-read styling, corporate marketing feel. No invented metrics, customers, or quotes.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The blog is a continuous fanfold listing. Tractor-feed sprocket rails own the frame and act as wayfinding; perforation folds mark section breaks. Refuses the card-grid blog and the dark neon "tech" page.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite edition (chosen 2026-11-04 over the light paper version, which read as not professional/IT enough). Canvas #0F1419, deep #0A0E12 for rails and footer, raised #161C24, row band #151C25, ink #E6EBF2, secondary #9AA6B6, one azure accent #4C8DFF for actions, links and the current position. No glow, no neon. Sprocket rails, dotted perforation folds, alternating index rows. Pretendard for reading and headings, D2Coding only in code. Covers are halftone prints: cool white dots on graphite with one azure tone.
STORY: The reader sees the newest printout, scans the index rows by tag/author/date, opens a post, reads a calm column, and resumes where they stopped.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: left sprocket rail full height; masthead wordmark set very large in one face (raise from alphabet storm); featured post as the top leaf with Higgsfield halftone cover; green-bar index rows below with title, excerpt, author, readTime, date. Primary action: open the featured post.
FORM: Candidate 4 of 7 (fanfold continuous printout). seed:1848a6eba351c9b6. Signature: the rail advances hole by hole with scroll and doubles as the post's table of contents; a folded tab flags where the reader stopped (raise from cutting bench). Every post carries a publish/update history line (raise from provenance ribbon).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
